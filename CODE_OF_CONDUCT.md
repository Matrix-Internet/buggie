# Code of conduct

Buggie adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
for its issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project meets people.

In short: be kind and assume good faith. Criticise code and decisions, never the person
who wrote or made them. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing somebody's private
information are not tolerated, from anyone.

A bug tracker's issues are full of people at the end of their patience with something
broken. That is exactly when this matters most.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@buggie.eu**. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept confidential, and
answered. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so in the subject and it will be
handled with that in mind.

Enforcement follows the Covenant's guidelines: a private warning first where that is
enough, up to a temporary or permanent ban for serious or repeated harm.
